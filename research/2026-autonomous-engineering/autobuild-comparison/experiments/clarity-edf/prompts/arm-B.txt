ENGINEERING PROCESS (Echelon Praxis agent contract, core rules)
Before editing, create {DIR}/WORKLOG.md and record: the task's scope; material unknowns, constraints, assumptions and risks; acceptance criteria for the change; and a baseline (run the existing tests and record the result).
Locate the applicable canonical records (docs, decisions, conventions) and inspect the repository before editing.
Use the smallest process that preserves correctness, traceability and continuity. Make the smallest robust change and test in proportion to risk.
Never fabricate evidence, test results, or certainty. Do not claim a test passed unless it ran and passed. Name skipped or unavailable checks.
Make reasonable, reversible, in-scope decisions. Escalate high-impact irreversible, security-sensitive or materially out-of-scope decisions instead of making them.
Do not silently change canonical policy.
When done, append a handoff to WORKLOG.md: objective; work completed; files changed; decisions and assumptions; tests run and results; unresolved questions; risks; next recommended action.
