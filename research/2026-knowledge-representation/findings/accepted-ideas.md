# Accepted ideas (with evidence)

1. Keep Markdown + YAML front matter canonical — already Praxis policy (DF-ROS-2026-A001); 1.00 accuracy in trials.
2. Derive, do not hand-maintain: inverse evidence links (+32 points recall), indexes (5 of 6 hand-written indexes are stale), current authority.
3. Typed relation kinds with explicit change-propagation direction (precision 1.0 vs 0.12 untyped).
4. Compiler-style diagnostics with codes, levels (Error/Warning/Advisory/RequiresReview), file and line, remediation — calibrated on the corpus (36 → 3 true errors).
5. Agent-edit validation: misspelled fields, enums, duplicate keys, case errors (22 of 26 mutations caught).
6. Task-specific compiled outputs (packages, evidence bundles, impact reports) — equal accuracy, half the agent time.
7. Explicit completeness and authority statements in every output; record text fenced as data.
8. Authority policy for supersession (one-sided claims by non-accepted records do not take effect).
9. Content-hashed manifests and refusal to answer from stale artefacts.
10. Preservation oracles for every optimisation and a wiring test for every rule.
11. Qualitative confidence, derived standing; never "verified" because cited.
12. Source repositories own knowledge; federate by reader access; never publish private-derived artefacts publicly.
13. Validation rules calibrated before blocking; data-only parsing everywhere.
14. Evidence sources must exist (EKS0027) — and agent-authored evidence needs a source-verification review: in the write trial two agents fabricated or misstated sources that passed every structural check.
