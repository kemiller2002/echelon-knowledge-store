The support team's Q3 2026 survey reports 2 refunds larger than the original entry
out of 1,240. Both were deliberate goodwill credits approved by finance. Support asks
to keep the ability to refund more than the original in exceptional cases and does
not consider over-refunds a problem. This runs against DF-FXL-0006, which caps refunds
at the unrefunded remainder and makes the ledger reject over-refunds.
