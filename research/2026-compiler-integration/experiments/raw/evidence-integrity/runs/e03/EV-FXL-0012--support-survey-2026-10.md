The Q3 2026 support survey reports 2 of 1,240 refunds larger than the original entry. Both were deliberate goodwill credits approved by finance, and reconciling them took 20 minutes in total. Support asks to keep the ability to refund more than the original in exceptional cases and does not consider over-refunds a problem. This runs against DF-FXL-0006, which has the ledger reject over-refunds.
