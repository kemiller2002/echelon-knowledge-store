status: accepted

source_uri: ../../sources/support-survey-2026-10.md
---

The support survey on Q3 2026 refunds (`sources/support-survey-2026-10.md`, prepared
2026-10-05) reports 1,240 refunds processed, of which 2 (0.16%) were larger than the
original entry. Both were deliberate goodwill credits approved by finance, taking
20 minutes to reconcile in total. Support staff asked to keep the ability to refund
more than the original in exceptional cases and do not consider over-refunds a
problem.

This contradicts DF-FXL-0006, which caps refunds at the unrefunded remainder and
ends over-refund handling. The survey does not support that decision.
