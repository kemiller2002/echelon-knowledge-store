Of 1,240 refunds processed in Q3 2026, 2 exceeded the original entry; both were
deliberate goodwill credits approved by finance, taking 20 minutes to reconcile.
Support staff asked to keep the ability to refund more than the original in
exceptional cases and do not consider over-refunds a problem. This runs against
the refund ceiling in DF-FXL-0006.
