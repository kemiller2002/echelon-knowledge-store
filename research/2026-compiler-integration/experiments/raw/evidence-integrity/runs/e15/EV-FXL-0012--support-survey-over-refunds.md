The Q3 2026 support survey (2026-10-05) reports 1,240 refunds, of which only 2
(0.16%) exceeded the original entry. Both were deliberate goodwill credits
approved by finance, and reconciling them took 20 minutes in total. Support
staff asked to keep the ability to refund more than the original in exceptional
cases and do not consider over-refunds a problem. This does not support the
refund ceiling in DF-FXL-0006; it contradicts it.
