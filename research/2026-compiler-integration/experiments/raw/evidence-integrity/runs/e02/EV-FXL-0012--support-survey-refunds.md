Only 2 of 1,240 Q3 refunds (0.16%) exceeded the original entry. Both were
deliberate goodwill credits approved by finance and took 20 minutes in total to
reconcile. Support asked to keep the ability to over-refund in exceptional
cases. This contradicts, rather than supports, the refund ceiling in DF-FXL-0006.
