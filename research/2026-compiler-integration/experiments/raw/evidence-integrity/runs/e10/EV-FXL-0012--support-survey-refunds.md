The Q3 2026 support survey reports 2 refunds larger than the original entry out
of 1,240 processed. Both were deliberate goodwill credits approved by finance.
Support does not consider over-refunds a problem and asks to keep the ability to
refund more than the original in exceptional cases, which conflicts with
DF-FXL-0006 (refunds capped at the unrefunded remainder).
