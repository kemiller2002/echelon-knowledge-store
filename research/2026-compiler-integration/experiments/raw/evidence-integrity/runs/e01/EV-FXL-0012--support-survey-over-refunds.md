The October 2026 support survey of Q3 refunds found 2 of 1,240 refunds (0.16%)
larger than the original entry. Both were deliberate goodwill credits approved by
finance, and reconciling them took 20 minutes in total. Support staff asked to keep
the ability to refund more than the original and do not consider over-refunds a
problem. This runs against the rationale of DF-FXL-0006, so it is recorded as
contradicting evidence, not supporting evidence.
