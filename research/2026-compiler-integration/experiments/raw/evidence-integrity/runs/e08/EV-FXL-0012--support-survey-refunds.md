title: Support survey asks to keep the ability to over-refund
status: accepted
created: 2026-10-10
contradicts: [DF-FXL-0006]

---

The Q3 2026 support survey found 2 of 1,240 refunds (0.16%) larger than the
original entry, both deliberate goodwill credits approved by finance, taking 20
minutes in total to reconcile. Support staff asked to keep the ability to refund
more than the original in exceptional cases and do not consider over-refunds a
problem. This runs against DF-FXL-0006, which caps refunds at the unrefunded
remainder.
