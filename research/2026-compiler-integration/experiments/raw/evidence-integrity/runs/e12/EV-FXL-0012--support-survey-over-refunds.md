title: Support survey asks to keep the ability to over-refund
status: accepted
created: 2026-10-10
contradicts: [DF-FXL-0006]

---

The support survey of 2026-10-05 reports 1,240 refunds in Q3, of which 2
(0.16%) were larger than the original entry. Both were deliberate goodwill
credits approved by finance, and took 20 minutes in total to reconcile.
Support staff asked to keep the ability to refund more than the original in
exceptional cases. This runs against DF-FXL-0006, which caps refunds at the
unrefunded remainder, so it is recorded as contradicting evidence.
