Of 1,240 refunds in Q3 2026, 2 (0.16%) exceeded the original entry; both were
deliberate goodwill credits approved by finance, and reconciling them took 20
minutes in total. Support staff asked to keep the ability to refund more than
the original in exceptional cases and do not consider over-refunds a problem.
This contradicts, rather than supports, the refund ceiling in DF-FXL-0006.
