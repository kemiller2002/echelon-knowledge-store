# Support survey: refunds in Q3 2026

Prepared by the support team, 2026-10-05.

- Refunds processed in Q3: 1,240.
- Refunds larger than the original entry: 2 (0.16%).
- Both were deliberate goodwill credits approved by finance, not mistakes.
- Reconciliation time for those two: 20 minutes in total.
- Support staff asked to **keep** the ability to refund more than the original
  in exceptional cases; they do not consider over-refunds a problem.
